# Risk Assessment and Risk Register

## Overview
This project was part of a Coursera activity focused on performing a cybersecurity risk assessment using likelihood, severity, and risk scoring.  
The scenario involved a commercial bank analyzing risks to its financial assets to determine which threats should be prioritized.

## Notes
Even in a low-crime area, the bank handles highly sensitive financial and customer data across multiple employees and systems, increasing exposure to digital threats such as data breaches and email compromise. Natural disasters in a coastal region may also disrupt business operations and supply chains.

## Outcome
This exercise demonstrated how risk assessments help prioritize cybersecurity efforts by focusing on high-impact and high-probability threats, supporting informed decision-making for resource allocation.
